## Research directions

<div class="feature-grid">
<div class="feature"><h3>Learning × model-based control</h3><p>Combine data-driven robot skills with optimization, dynamics, and feedback control for physical execution.</p></div>
<div class="feature"><h3>Actuator-aware sim-to-real</h3><p>Connect motor and drivetrain behavior, identification, simulation, and real-time policy deployment.</p></div>
<div class="feature"><h3>Humanoid whole-body motion</h3><p>Study the coordination of locomotion, balance, and precise upper-body behavior on real humanoids.</p></div>
<div class="feature"><h3>Human–robot interaction</h3><p>Build on earlier work in wearable robotics, soft sensing, and robust measurement of human motion.</p></div>

## Research experience

**Korea Institute of Machinery and Materials (KIMM)** · Researcher, Advanced Robotics Research Center  
Developed KAIROS v0.5's locomotion policy and was responsible for both demonstration robots at KIMM's 50th anniversary; developed locomotion and dancing policies for one KAIROS robot at the 2026 Global Forum on Mechanical Engineering. Ongoing work in sim-to-real and humanoid control.

**Agency for Defense Development (ADD)** · Ground Technology Research Institute  
Research in motion planning, nonlinear model predictive control, and engineering optimization.

**KAIST EXO Lab** · Researcher, 2023–2024  
Wearable robotics, robust control, pneumatic mechanomyography, and soft sensing.

**Dyze Design** · Student intern, Montréal, January–May 2017

# Seongbin An

I am a robotics researcher at the **Korea Institute of Machinery and Materials (KIMM)**, working in the Advanced Robotics Research Center. My research focuses on humanoid control, combining reinforcement learning with model-based methods to bring robot behavior from simulation to hardware.

My experience spans control algorithms, actuator dynamics, simulation, and real-time robot systems. Earlier, I worked on wearable robots and soft sensing at KAIST's EXO Lab and conducted research at the Agency for Defense Development (ADD).

## Research Interests

- Learning-based locomotion and sim-to-real transfer for humanoid robots
- Model-based and predictive control for coordinated locomotion and manipulation
- Actuator modeling, system identification, and robust real-time control
- Human–robot interaction, wearable robotics, and soft sensing
